# Direct-Response Copywriting Frameworks
**Target Platform:** Mobile Social Feeds (Meta/TikTok)
**Scope:** Structural blueprints for high-converting ad copy rewrites.
**Global Directive:** Frameworks must dictate the invisible psychological flow of the copy. NEVER print the framework labels (e.g., "Problem:", "Attention:") in the final output. 

## The PAS Framework (Problem, Agitate, Solve)
**Diagnostic Triggers:** `rewrite_pas`, `low_ctr`, `pain_point_product`
**Cognitive Mechanism:** Negativity Bias. Humans are wired to pay more attention to threats and pain than to positive aspirations. PAS leverages this by starting with a relatable frustration, twisting the knife to highlight the emotional cost of ignoring it, and presenting the product as the immediate relief.
**The Mechanical Rule:**
1. **Problem (Characters 0-80):** Call out a highly specific, painful daily frustration. 
2. **Agitate (Characters 80-150):** Explain the consequence of leaving this problem unsolved, or why other solutions have failed.
3. **Solve:** Introduce the product as the exact antidote, followed by the CTA.
**Rewrite Exemplar:**
- *Before (Feature focus):* Our new accounting software features automated expense tracking and one-click invoice generation for small businesses.
- *After (PAS execution):* Spending your weekends chasing down missing client invoices? (Problem) 
  Every unpaid invoice is cash flow you are bleeding this month. (Agitate) 
  Automate your follow-ups and get paid in 24 hours with our one-click invoicing tool. Start free today. (Solve)

## The BAB Framework (Before, After, Bridge)
**Diagnostic Triggers:** `rewrite_bab`, `transformation_product`, `aspirational`
**Cognitive Mechanism:** The Gap Theory. People do not buy products; they buy a better version of themselves. BAB paints a picture of their current bleak reality, contrasts it with a vivid picture of the promised land, and positions your product as the only logical bridge between the two. Best for fitness, wealth, and beauty offers.
**The Mechanical Rule:**
1. **Before:** State the user's current undesirable state.
2. **After:** Describe the user's life with the problem completely eliminated. 
3. **Bridge:** How your specific product gets them from Before to After.
**Rewrite Exemplar:**
- *Before (Boring description):* Join our 6-week online coding bootcamp. We teach Python, React, and SQL. Sign up now to start your tech career.
- *After (BAB execution):* Stuck in a dead-end job checking the clock until 5 PM? (Before)
  Imagine logging in remotely, writing code that matters, and earning a developer's salary. (After)
  Our 6-week bootcamp is the fastest bridge to your first tech job. See the curriculum below. (Bridge)

## The AIDA Framework (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
**Diagnostic Triggers:** `rewrite_aida`, `brand_awareness`, `e-commerce`
**Cognitive Mechanism:** The Sales Funnel Micro-Dose. This is the oldest, most reliable framework for e-commerce products. It grabs the user by the collar, hooks them with a fascinating mechanism, builds social proof, and demands a click.
**The Mechanical Rule:**
1. **Attention:** A scroll-stopping hook (must be <125 characters).
2. **Interest:** A surprising fact, unique mechanism, or counter-intuitive statement about the product.
3. **Desire:** Inject social proof or a massive benefit that triggers FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out).
4. **Action:** A clear, frictionless directive to click the button.
**Rewrite Exemplar:**
- *Before:* We sell premium noise-canceling headphones with 40 hours of battery life and bluetooth connectivity. Order yours today.
- *After (AIDA execution):* The headphones audio engineers actually use on flights. (Attention)
  Our dual-chip ANC cancels 98% of airplane engine roar instantly. (Interest)
  Over 50,000 frequent flyers swear by them for deep sleep on long hauls. (Desire)
  Grab yours for 15% off before the holiday rush. (Action)

## Feature-to-Benefit Translation Matrix
**Diagnostic Triggers:** `is_wall_of_text=True`, `tech_specs_heavy`, `boring_copy`
**Cognitive Mechanism:** Outcome-Based Processing. Customers do not care about the "mechanics" of a product; they care about the "meaning" of the product. Listing technical specifications forces the customer to do the mental math of why it matters. 
**The Mechanical Rule:**
1. Never list a feature on its own.
2. Force the translation using the **"So You Can..."** rule. Connect the physical feature to an emotional, financial, or time-saving outcome.
3. Format as punchy bullet points to break up text walls.
**Rewrite Exemplar:**
- *Before (Specs):* 
  • 256GB SSD storage
  • 24-hour battery life
  • Water-resistant coating
- *After (Benefits):* 
  • 256GB Storage: Keep 100,000 photos without ever seeing a "Storage Full" warning.
  • 24-Hr Battery: Leave your charger at home for the entire weekend.
  • Water-Resistant: Survive coffee spills and sudden rainstorms.
